Asset Trackers · Powered and unpowered

Every asset. One map.

Track trailers and equipment right alongside your vehicles.

Trailers sit idle, equipment wanders off, and cargo and jobsite theft keep climbing. Netradyne Asset Tracking puts trailers, equipment, dumpsters and containers on the same map as your fleet, in the same dashboard you already use. Set geofences, get alerts the moment something leaves, and find what's misplaced before it becomes a loss.

Powered trackerBuilt for trailers and forklifts. Solar-rechargeable backup keeps it reporting with trip tracking, GPS and data logging.
Unpowered trackerBuilt for dumpsters, containers and roll-offs. Self-powered with a long-life battery, and it installs in minutes.

Fuel Management

Find the fuel you're wasting.

Know whether it's the driver or the vehicle, and fix it.

Fuel is one of the biggest lines on your P&L, and most of the waste hides in everyday habits like idling, hard acceleration and hard braking. Netradyne connects fuel use to driving behavior, with video for context, so you can see exactly what's driving cost. Then you can coach on it, and drivers can track their own MPG and idle time right in the app.

  • Fuel use, idling and driving behavior in one view
  • Fuel-wasting alerts with integrated video for full context
  • Drivers see their MPG, idle percentage and average speed in the app
  • Recognize and reward drivers who improve

Tracking and Weather

Know where they are, and what they're driving into.

Real-time GPS tracking with live weather on the same map.

Knowing where a truck is only helps if you know what's ahead of it. Netradyne shows every vehicle in real time, with event video along the route, geofences and location sharing for your customers. Active Weather layers National Weather Service alerts, radar and forecasts onto the fleet map. You can see which drivers are in the storm's path, then reroute, delay or check in before conditions turn.

  • Real-time GPS tracking with geofences and customer location sharing
  • Active Weather Overlay: NWS alert zones, radar, forecasts and impacted vehicles
  • Speed for Conditions gives drivers earlier weather awareness
  • Overlay and Speed for Conditions included at no extra cost for US customers†

Maintenance

Fix it before it breaks down.

Preventive maintenance driven by real vehicle data.

Unplanned downtime costs more than the repair. It costs the load, the schedule and the customer. Netradyne brings engine faults, diagnostics, idling trends and inspection results into one cloud platform. Automated workflows make sure the right fix happens at the right time. When a driver flags a defect, the mechanic knows right away.

  • Engine fault codes and vehicle diagnostics in one dashboard
  • Custom workflows automate preventive maintenance
  • Driver-friendly DVIRs notify the mechanic when a repair is needed
  • Flag vehicles with critical defects and catch missed inspections
Keep more trucks on the road

ELD and Compliance

Now ELD certified. One device for safety and compliance.

Your Driver•i camera is also your FMCSA-registered ELD.

Why put two devices, two apps and two vendors in the cab? The Driver•i camera that protects your drivers also logs their hours. It's registered with the FMCSA under the agency's newer, more rigorous vetting. HOS, DVIR and IFTA all run on the same platform as your safety program, with less paperwork and fewer surprises at roadside.

  • FMCSA-registered ELD built into the Driver•i camera
  • HOS, DVIR and IFTA on one platform
  • One device, one driver app, one vendor
  • Manage driver logs, availability and violations across the fleet
